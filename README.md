# 💸 FreelanceAI – AI Freelancer Finance Assistant

A smart finance management platform built for freelancers to simplify invoicing, estimate earnings, compare payment processing fees, and receive AI-powered financial guidance.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://ai-freelancer-finance-assistant.vercel.app  
📂 **GitHub Repository:** https://github.com/codebymahima/AI-freelancer-finance-assistant

---

## 🚀 Overview

Freelancers often struggle with managing invoices, tracking earnings, understanding payment processor fees, and making informed financial decisions. FreelanceAI provides an all-in-one solution that combines financial tools with AI-powered assistance to help freelancers manage their business finances more effectively.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🔐 Secure Authentication
- User signup and login using Supabase Authentication
- Secure access to personal financial data
- User-specific invoice management

### 📊 Financial Dashboard
- Track total earnings
- Monitor pending payments
- View invoice statistics
- Analyze monthly income at a glance

### 🧾 Invoice Generator
- Create professional invoices
- Add client information and billing details
- Include multiple service line items
- Automatic total calculation
- Save invoices to the database
- Download invoices as PDF

### 📁 Invoice Management
- View invoice history
- Search invoices by client details
- Track payment status
- Mark invoices as paid
- Download previously created invoices
- Delete invoices when required

### 📈 Earnings Estimator
- Calculate projected freelance income
- Estimate weekly, monthly, and annual earnings
- Adjust tax rates and business expenses
- Understand expected take-home income

### 💳 Payment Fee Calculator
Compare popular international payment methods:
- PayPal
- Stripe
- Wise
- Payoneer
- Square
- Bank Wire Transfer

Features:
- Fee comparison
- Net payout calculation
- Best-value payment recommendation

### 🤖 AI Finance Assistant
- Ask freelancer-specific finance questions
- Get concise and practical financial guidance
- Compare international payment options
- Understand pricing strategies
- Learn about invoicing and income management
- AI response fallback system for improved reliability
